**Linux** — `refryt-0.2.0-x86_64.AppImage`: download, `chmod +x`, run. Or use
`scripts/appimage-install.sh install` from source for a desktop launcher entry.

**Linux, Flatpak** — `flatpak install --user https://gridoe.github.io/refryt/refryt.flatpakref`,
then `flatpak run io.github.gridoe.refryt`; updates come with `flatpak update`. The file
`refryt-0.2.0-x86_64.flatpak` installs the same build and updates from the same repo. It
needs the GNOME 51 runtime, which Flatpak offers to fetch the first time. mpv and yt-dlp are
inside. The sandbox can't see your browsers' profile folders, so "Import from a browser" is hidden
and you sign in through the app's own window; "Launch on startup" is hidden too, since a sandboxed
app can't write a login item.

**Windows** — three options for x64, and two for ARM, cross-compiled from Linux with limited testing on real Windows
hardware:
- `refryt-0.2.0-windows-x86_64-setup.exe` — a real installer (Program Files, Start Menu,
  uninstaller). Unsigned, so SmartScreen will warn on first run — expected, not a bug.
- `refryt-0.2.0-windows-x86_64.msi` — the same files as an MSI, for deployment tools or a
  silent install (`msiexec /i refryt-0.2.0-windows-x86_64.msi /qn`). It installs to the same
  folder as the setup, so use one or the other.
- `refryt-0.2.0-windows-x86_64.zip` — portable, no install: unzip and run `refryt.exe`.
  Extract the whole folder before running; the exe needs the DLLs beside it.
- `refryt-0.2.0-windows-arm64.msi` / `.zip` — a native build for Windows on ARM, not yet tested
  on ARM hardware. The x64 files also run there under emulation.

**macOS** — `refryt-0.2.0-macos-arm64.dmg`, for Apple Silicon only. Open it and drag
Refryt to Applications. It is signed ad hoc and not notarized, so
macOS will refuse to open it on the first launch. Run `xattr -cr /Applications/Refryt.app` once,
or allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security. libmpv and yt-dlp are bundled; nothing needs
installing.

Checksums for every file are in `refryt-0.2.0-SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Already on the Flatpak from a downloaded file?** Files up to 0.1.14 were not linked to the
repo, so `flatpak update` never finds a new version for them. Move over once; your settings and
sign-in stay:

```
flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists refryt https://gridoe.github.io/refryt/refryt.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user --reinstall refryt io.github.gridoe.refryt
```

From then on `flatpak update` keeps it current.

What changed since 0.1.14:

- **Up next, in two parts** — the queue now separates the songs you chose from the ones the
  radio picked:
  - **Next up** holds what you queued with Play next or Add to queue, in the order they will
    play. A small ✕ beside the heading clears them; the playing song and the radio stay.
  - **Autoplay** holds the radio YouTube Music keeps adding after your songs. With nothing of
    your own queued, the list is one "Next up" section.
  - **Radio filters** — the "All ▾" menu on Autoplay offers the filters YouTube Music gives
    that radio: Familiar, Discover, and often a decade or a genre. Picking one swaps the
    Autoplay songs still to come; the playing song and your own queued songs are kept. Some
    radios come with no filters, and then there is no menu.
  - **Add to queue during autoplay** — a song added while a radio song was playing used to land
    behind the playing song, so it never showed in Up next and never played. It now goes after
    your queued songs and before Autoplay. Adding a song that is already in Autoplay, with
    Add to queue or Play next, moves it up instead of queuing it twice.
  - **Shuffle part way through** — turning shuffle on no longer deals the songs you already
    played back in as upcoming. They stay behind the playing song, where Previous still reaches
    them; only what is still to come is shuffled.
  - **The radio survives a restart** — reopening refryt keeps which songs were yours, where the
    radio continues from, and its filters, instead of treating the whole saved queue as yours.
  - **Mixes play as a radio** — My Mix, Discover Mix and a song's or playlist's radio play the
    way YouTube Music plays them: they keep going past their first page and bring Autoplay's
    filters. This works from a mix's card and from its page's Play button.
  - The queue's rows are tighter, and shrink their number column and cover in a narrow window.
- **Sign-in fix** — importing a sign-in could fail with "YouTube rejected the cookie… Error
  parsing header" when the browser stored the SAPISID cookie last. The order no longer matters.
  When YouTube still refuses a session, the log says where it was sent (a Google sign-in or
  check, the robot check or the consent page), without any cookie values, and the error says
  what to do.
- **Corners** — Settings > Appearance has Corners: Round (as before), Soft and Sharp. Panels,
  popups, rows and covers follow it; chips, toggles and the search box stay round. On Linux the
  window's own corner stays small at every level, so a tiled or split window does not look
  over-rounded.
- **Album and playlist extras** — an album shows "Other versions" and "Releases for you" under
  its tracks, and someone else's playlist shows "Related playlists", as on YouTube Music.
- **Release cards** — cards for albums, singles and EPs on Home now name the artist and the
  type ("LISA • Single"). Any card whose title or subtitle is cut off shows both in full on
  hover. Hover hints have a lighter shadow.
- **Card buttons** — a card's play button pauses or resumes its album, playlist or song when
  that is what is loaded, instead of starting it over.
- **Smaller fixes** — scrolling titles hold at their end before going back; a button no longer
  stays pressed after dragging or resizing the window; the update notice gives Homebrew and
  Scoop installs their own upgrade command.
- **Privacy** — PRIVACY.md lists what refryt stores and what each service it contacts receives;
  the Windows installer shows it before installing. Settings > Integrations has "Synced lyrics
  from LRCLIB", on by default; off, lyrics come from YouTube alone, without timing.
